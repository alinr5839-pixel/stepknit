Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

This policy explains how StepKnit ("we", "the factory") handles information when you visit stepknit.com and contact us. We keep data collection to the minimum needed to answer inquiries and run our business.

1. Information we collect

  • Contact details you give us — when you message us on WhatsApp, call, or email, we receive your name, country, phone/email and the content of your enquiry. This page itself does not store form data on a server; submitting the form opens WhatsApp/email on your device.
  • Usage data — our website and analytics tools (Google Analytics 4 and Meta Pixel) may set cookies to measure visits, ad performance and conversions. These are enabled only after you accept in the cookie banner.

2. How we use information

  • To reply to enquiries, send catalogues and quotations, and process orders.
  • To measure and improve our website and advertising (Google and Meta ads).
  • To comply with legal and customs obligations for exports.

3. Legal basis (GDPR)

Where the GDPR applies, we process data on the basis of your consent (analytics/marketing cookies), the performance of a contract or pre-contractual steps (responding to enquiries and orders), and our legitimate interest in running and improving our business.

4. Cookies & consent

We use a cookie consent banner. Non-essential analytics and advertising cookies are only loaded after you click "Accept". You can withdraw consent at any time by clearing your browser cookies. We do not sell personal data.

5. Sharing

We share information only with service providers we rely on (e.g. hosting, analytics, advertising platforms, freight forwarders and payment providers) as needed to serve you, and where required by law.

6. International transfers

Our factory is in China and our services (hosting, analytics, ads) may process data outside your country. We rely on the platforms' standard safeguards for such transfers.

7. Data retention & your rights

We keep enquiry data only as long as needed. You may request access, correction, deletion or to object to processing. You also have the right to complain to your local data-protection authority.
