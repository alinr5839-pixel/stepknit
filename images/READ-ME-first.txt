SHOOTING TIPS FOR YOUR OWN PHOTOS
---------------------------------
* Product shots: white or light grey background, shoe at a 3/4 angle, one
  pair per photo, shoot near a window or under a soft light, square crop.
* Factory shots: turn the lights on, shoot down the machine rows, include
  workers' hands in frame, landscape orientation.
* Keep real brand names and other customers' logos out of the frame.
* Compress to under 300 KB per photo (JPEG quality 80) — the page is opened
  mostly on mobile data in Ghana.
* If you prefer different filenames, open index.html and change the
  matching src="images/xxx.jpg" line.
